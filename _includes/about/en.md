> About the investing posts

These posts document **my own research, judgment, real trades and the way my trading system evolves**.

I'm not a licensed investment advisor, and none of this **constitutes investment advice**. Details of my actual positions are anonymized; price levels cited are public market data.

I'm especially willing to write up the cases where I got the direction right but the execution wrong — or simply lost money. Those are the most useful posts for me, and I suspect for readers too.
